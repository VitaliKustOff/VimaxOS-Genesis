# Diamond Certification

A GEM becomes a Diamond only after all mandatory criteria are satisfied.

1. The main human scenario works on a real target.
2. The result survives restart, update, and ordinary failure conditions.
3. Critical data is preserved or recoverable.
4. Declared capabilities correspond to real capabilities.
5. No component acts beyond its nature.
6. Positive and negative experience are documented.
7. The organism conforms to Constitution and GDPoV.
8. Experience is returned to Genesis.
9. A human owner approves promotion.

A successful compilation, demo, screenshot, or test suite alone does not grant Diamond status.
