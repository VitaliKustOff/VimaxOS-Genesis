# GDPoV Phases

## GDPoV-00 — Intent
A human formulates a real need.

## GDPoV-01 — Meaning
The need is transformed into a semantic description.

## GDPoV-02 — Natural Capability
Every entity declares only real capabilities and limitations.

## GDPoV-03 — DNA
The organism receives a machine-readable and human-readable semantic passport.

## GDPoV-04 — GEN
Genesis derives organs, molecules, dependencies, natural tools, and implementation targets from DNA.

## GDPoV-05 — GEMS
The product enters development and testing as a GEM.

## GDPoV-06 — Evolution
Every iteration records hypothesis, change, result, positive experience, negative experience, and next decision.

## GDPoV-07 — Verification
The organism is checked against Constitution, Paradigm, DNA, natural capabilities, safety, and first-success criteria.

## GDPoV-08 — Diamond Promotion
Promotion is permitted only after proven real-world usefulness and stability.

## GDPoV-09 — Experience Integration
Both positive and negative experience are returned to Genesis. Nothing useful is forgotten.
