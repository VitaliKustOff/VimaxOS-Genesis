# GDPoV Required Artifacts

Every GEM must contain:

- `INTENT.md`
- `MEANING.md`
- `NATURE.yaml`
- `DNA.yaml`
- `CAPABILITIES.yaml`
- `LIMITATIONS.md`
- `ITERATIONS.md`
- `EXPERIENCE.md`
- `VERIFICATION.md`
- `STATUS.yaml`

A Diamond must additionally contain:

- `DIAMOND-CERTIFICATE.md`
- `PROMOTION-RECORD.yaml`
- `EXPERIENCE-EXPORT.yaml`
- reproducible build instructions
- release artifact checksums
- known limitations
- rollback or safe-failure procedure

## Experience signs

- `experience:+` — confirmed useful decision
- `experience:-` — failed, unsafe, ineffective, or misleading decision

Negative experience is not deleted. It becomes a prohibition, warning, boundary, or alternative.
