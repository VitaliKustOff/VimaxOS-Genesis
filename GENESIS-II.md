# Genesis II

The VimaxOS core already exists as an idea and initial architecture.

Genesis II does not replace it. It materializes the existing principles into stable contracts:

- shared identity;
- contextual roles;
- trust decisions;
- modular capabilities;
- events;
- synchronization.

`Vimax.Nail` becomes the first integrated organ and the first proof that two people on two devices can act through one Vimax state.
