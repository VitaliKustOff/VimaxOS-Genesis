# GDPoV Terminology

## Chaos
Absence of sufficient structure, certainty, or meaningful relationships. Chaos is not evil.

## Cosmos
A meaningfully ordered state in which entities occupy natural places and act within real capabilities.

## Meaning
The bridge from Chaos to Cosmos.

## GEM
A VIMAX organism under development, testing, correction, and refinement.

## Diamond
A proven VIMAX organism whose usefulness, stability, boundaries, and experience are verified.

## GEN
The act or mechanism by which Genesis derives structure and embodiment from DNA.

## Nature
The real properties, limits, and abilities of an entity.

## Natural Fit
The degree to which a tool is appropriate for a task by nature, not merely technically usable.
